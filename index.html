Emergency Root Canal

Do You Need an Emergency Root Canal in Stockton?

When the nerve inside a tooth becomes infected or inflamed, the pain can be severe and constant. An emergency root canal removes the infection, relieves the pressure and helps you keep your natural tooth instead of losing it.

  • Severe, throbbing or constant tooth pain
  • Lingering sensitivity to hot or cold
  • Swollen gums or facial swelling
  • A pimple-like bump on the gum
  • A darkened or discolored tooth
  • Pain when biting or chewing

FAQs

Emergency Root Canal & Urgent Dental Care FAQs

Quick answers to the questions Stockton patients ask us most.

When do I need an emergency root canal?

You may need an emergency root canal if you have severe or throbbing tooth pain, lingering sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling in the gums or face, a pimple-like bump on the gum, or a tooth that has darkened. These can be signs of an infected tooth nerve. Call 209-490-2698 for urgent evaluation.

Is an emergency root canal painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so the treated area is numb. The goal of a root canal is to remove the source of your pain, and most patients find the procedure itself comparable to getting a filling.

Are you really open 24 hours in Stockton?

Yes. We are a 24 hour emergency dentist serving Stockton, CA. Call 209-490-2698 any time, day or night, and our team will help you arrange urgent dental care.

Can I get a same day dental appointment?

We focus on urgent dental appointments and same day care for emergencies such as severe toothache, dental abscess, broken or chipped teeth and dental trauma. Call us as early as possible so we can fit you in.

What should I do while waiting for my emergency dental visit?

Rinse gently with warm salt water, apply a cold compress to the outside of your cheek to reduce swelling, avoid very hot, cold or hard foods, and take over-the-counter pain relief as directed on the label. Do not place aspirin directly on the gum. If you have facial swelling with fever, or trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room.
